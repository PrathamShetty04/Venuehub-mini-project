Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for customers and venue owners.

Customer FAQ

How do I find a venue?

Open Browse Venues, review the available halls, and use the filters to narrow your options.

How do I request a booking?

Open a venue, choose Book, complete the event details and submit the request. You can review its status in your customer dashboard.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your request remains pending until the venue owner accepts or rejects it. Check your dashboard for the latest status.

What if I cannot sign in?

Use Forgot password on the login page. On the local demo, a one-time reset link is shown if email delivery has not been configured.

Can I cancel a booking?

You can cancel your own upcoming pending or confirmed booking from My Bookings.

When can I review a venue?

After your event has taken place and the owner marks the booking completed, you can submit one rating and review for that booking.

How is a per-plate booking priced?

The estimated total is the per-plate price multiplied by the number of guests. The guest count must be within the venue's listed capacity.

Owner FAQ

How do I list a venue?

Create an owner account, sign in, open your Owner Dashboard and select Add Venue. Include accurate details and a publicly accessible image URL.

How do I handle booking requests?

In Booking Requests, review each pending request and choose Accept or Reject. Once the event date has passed, mark a confirmed booking completed to enable the customer's review.

What happens when I remove a venue?

Removing a venue hides it from public listings. Existing booking history is retained.

Why is my venue image not appearing?

Check that the URL opens the image directly, uses HTTPS where supported, and does not require a login or block external embedding.
